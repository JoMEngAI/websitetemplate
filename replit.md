# Joseph Mendoza — Resume Website

Static single-page resume site (HTML/CSS/JS, Materialize CSS from CDN) for the
SWE 325/525 Career-Fair assignment. Final hosting is GitHub Pages, not Replit.

## Run
Workflow "Start application": `python3 -m http.server 5000 --bind 0.0.0.0`.

## Rules from the owner
- Never invent facts; the resume PDF is the factual baseline.
- Do not publish phone, email, or location, or upload a resume PDF, without approval.
- Keep asset paths relative (GitHub Pages subpath).
- The owner commits, pushes, and opens PRs himself through GUI tools; do not push or merge.
- Private uploads in `attached_assets/` are gitignored and must stay out of the repo.
